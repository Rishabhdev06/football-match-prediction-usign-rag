# English Premier League: Competition Structure and Historical Context

## Competition Format
- The English Premier League (EPL) consists of 20 clubs playing 38 match fixtures each season (19 home, 19 away).
- **Points System**:
  - Win: 3 points
  - Draw: 1 point
  - Loss: 0 points
- Standings are determined by total points, followed by goal difference, then total goals scored, and head-to-head records if tied.

## Match Outcomes and Home Advantage
- Over historical Premier League seasons (including 2022-2024):
  - **Home Wins** historically represent approximately 44% to 48% of all match outcomes.
  - **Draws** occur in approximately 22% to 26% of matches.
  - **Away Wins** account for approximately 28% to 32% of matches.
- Home advantage is influenced by familiar pitch dimensions, home crowd psychological support, lack of travel fatigue, and referee unconscious bias toward the home team.
